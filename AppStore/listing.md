## Description (4000 max)

Is $6.99 a good price for a kilogram of chicken thighs? PerGram Go answers in one word.

Type a shelf price, or point your camera at the tag, and get an instant verdict: good, meh, or bad. Everything is normalized to $/100g, so a 680g pack and a 1.4kg pack finally become quickly comparable.

SCAN THE TAG
Point the camera at a shelf tag and the fields fill themselves. It reads the printed unit price where there is one, works out the price per kilo from a random-weight meat label, understands "2 for $5", and reads English and French tags.

SET YOUR OWN GOOD PRICE
The verdict is measured against what you decide a good price is, not against an average. Set one straight from a check, or use what you paid at the end of a trip. An item can have one per weight, per volume, and each.

SHOP WITH A LIST
Make grocery lists sorted by aisle, with notes like "6" or "ripe ones". Uncheck what you're skipping this week; it stays on the list for next time. Shop the list with a running total, and items cross themselves off as they go in the cart. Finish a trip to see what you spent, how it rated, and the bad buys by name.

YOUR ITEMS
Everything you buy in one place, by aisle. Search knows hundreds of everyday Canadian groceries in English and French, and anything else can be typed.

SHARE A LIST
Send a list by AirDrop or Messages. Share it again after a change, and the other person sees exactly what changed before they update their copy.
