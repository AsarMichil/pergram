## Description (4000 max)

Is this actually a good price? PerGram Go answers that in the aisle.

Shelf prices aren't comparable as printed: a 680 g pack at $8.99 and a 1.4 kg pack at $16.49 are different prices per kilogram. Type the price and the size, or point the camera at the shelf tag, and PerGram Go turns it into a unit price — per 100 g, per kilogram, per pound, per litre, or each — and tells you whether it's good, meh, or bad.

Good against what? Against your own good price. You decide what chicken thighs or a dozen eggs should cost, and every check is measured against that, not someone else's average.

CHECK A PRICE
• Type it on a big keypad, or scan the shelf tag — English or French, per-pound and per-100 g tags included.
• Switch the display between $/100g, $/kg, $/lb and $/oz with a tap.
• Bookmark one price and check the next to see the difference.

SHOP WITH A LIST
• Make grocery lists, sorted by aisle, with notes for how many or which.
• Mark things "not this time" when you are sitting them out this week. They stay on the list for next time.
• Shop a list and keep a running total. Items cross themselves off as they go in the cart.
• Finish a trip to see what you spent, how it rated, and what you missed.

YOUR ITEMS
• Everything you buy in one place, by aisle.
• One good price per way it's sold: an avocado can have a good price each and per pound.
• No good price yet? Set one from a check, or use what you paid on your last trip.

SHARE A LIST
• Send a list by AirDrop or Messages. Re-share it after a change, and the other person sees exactly what changed.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking, and no network connection needed.
• Camera frames are read on your iPhone and never stored or sent anywhere.
• Prices are yours. PerGram Go has no store price database, so nothing is sold, scraped or shared.

Made in Canada, for Canadian groceries.
