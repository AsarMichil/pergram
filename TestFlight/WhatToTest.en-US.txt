PerGram Go — a unit-price sanity checker for Canadian grocery shopping. Get a price into it any way you like, and it answers one question: good, meh, or bad.

New in 1.2.0: grocery lists, shopping trips with a running cart, and Your items.

Before you start: if you installed an earlier 1.2.0 test build, delete the app and reinstall. Lists made with that build will not carry over.

What to try:
• Lists — make a list and add things by typing. Pick a suggestion, or type anything ("PC thighs" is fine). Each lands in its aisle.
• Tap something on a list to add a note ("6", "2 lb", "ripe ones"), rename it or move it to another aisle.
• Shop this list — switch off what you are skipping this week, then Start. Skips stay on the list for next time.
• On the Shop page, tap an item to price it. You land in Type or Scan with it already selected. Add it to the cart and it crosses itself off.
• Tap the circle to cross something off without pricing it. Swipe right on a row for "Not this time".
• Add the same item at the same price twice. It should count up to × 2, not add a second row.
• Add a price with no item picked, then swipe right on it in the cart and choose "What was this?".
• Pick an item with no good price on Check. Where the verdict would be, tap "Set as good price".
• Finish a trip. The summary names bad buys and offers "Use $X" for anything you bought without a good price.
• Your items (the Items tab) — everything you buy, by aisle. Tap one to set or remove good prices; an item can have one per weight, per volume and each.
• Share a list with another tester (AirDrop or Messages). Change it, share it again, and check the import screen: what changed, then Update or Save as copy.

Most useful feedback: anything that felt confusing, an item the search did not know, an item that landed in the wrong aisle, or a tag the scanner read wrong (with a photo).

Known limits:
• The cart total is what you checked, before tax and deposits — not a receipt.
• Sharing sends a copy. Lists do not sync live between phones.
• Search knows about 550 common Canadian groceries in English and French; anything else can be typed.
• Camera frames are processed on your device and never stored or sent anywhere.
